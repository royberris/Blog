# Umbraco Community Day 2023

*Originally published on the old Berris.dev blog in December 2023 and moved here with its original text. The photos from the original post didn't survive the move.*

On the 13th of December I attended Umbraco Community Day in person as a speaker and just hanging out behind the scenes.

## Building a Strong Foundation for your Modern .NET Project

I did a talk about the modern .NET and how the technology industry is shifting to a more composable way of working. Instead of working monolithic and using best of breed enterprise solutions we are composing smaller more targeted integrations with our existing solutions.
